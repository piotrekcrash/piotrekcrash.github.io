This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which the application Rclone ("the Application"), developed by Piotr Łyczko ("Developer", "we", "our"), accesses, collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users through Google APIs.

1. Google User Data We Access and Collect

When you authorize the Application to interact with your Google Account, we access the following types of Google user data:

  • Google Account Tokens: OAuth 2.0 access tokens and refresh tokens necessary to authenticate your requests against Google APIs.
  • Google Drive Files and Metadata: Directory structures, file names, file contents, and modification dates within your Google Drive, strictly bounded by the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.

2. How We Use Google User Data

Our application limits the use of data strictly to providing user-facing features. Specifically:

  • We use your data solely to execute command-line file operations (synchronization, backup, upload, and download) initiated directly by you.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements, personalized advertising, or retargeting.
  • We do not use Google user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not use Google user data to train, test, or fine-tune artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

3. Sharing and Transfer of Google User Data

We do not sell, rent, lease, or transfer Google user data to third parties under any circumstances.

  • No Data Brokers: We do not sell or disclose Google user data to data brokers or advertising networks.
  • Direct Connection: All API communications occur directly between your host machine running the Application and Google servers. We do not operate intermediate third-party proxy servers that receive or inspect your content.

4. Protection and Security Mechanisms for Sensitive Data

We implement technical and organizational security procedures to protect the confidentiality and integrity of your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transferred between your client and Google is encrypted using secure HTTPS / TLS protocols.
  • Local Storage: Authentication credentials and configuration files are stored locally on your own filesystem. No user data is transmitted to external servers maintained by the Developer.
