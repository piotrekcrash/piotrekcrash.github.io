Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the personal integration application Rclone ("the Application", "we", "our") accesses, collects, uses, and protects your information when interacting with Google services.

1. Information We Access and Collect

The Application accesses Google APIs solely to perform file synchronization, backup, and transfer operations initiated directly by the user. Specifically:

  • Google Drive Files and Metadata: The Application accesses files, folders, and metadata in your Google Drive solely to upload, download, move, or sync files as instructed by your local Rclone commands.
  • OAuth Authentication Tokens: The Application receives and stores authentication tokens (access and refresh tokens) to maintain authorization to your Google account.

2. How We Store and Protect Your Data

  • Local Storage Only: All authentication tokens, configuration profiles, and downloaded files are stored strictly on your local device or machine where Rclone is executed.
  • No External Servers: We do not operate external web servers or centralized databases that collect, transmit, or store your Google credentials, personal data, or synced files.

3. How We Use and Share Information

  • We only use Google user data to provide the core synchronization and file management functionality requested by the user.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data to third parties.
  • We do not share Google user data with any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics providers.
  • We do not use Google user data for training general artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Google Limited Use Disclosure:

The Application's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can revoke the Application's access to your Google account at any time via the Google Account Security Settings. You may also delete all locally stored tokens and configurations at any time by removing the remote configuration using the local command (e.g. rclone config delete [remote_name]) or deleting the local rclone.conf file.
